Glass has long been a medium of revelation and concealment. In contemporary architecture, it serves as the ultimate boundary dissolver, creating spaces that are simultaneously interior and exterior.

## The Evolution of Glass

From the stained glass of cathedrals to the towering curtain walls of modern skyscrapers, glass has evolved from a decorative element to a structural powerhouse. Today's architectural glass can be self-tinting, energy-generating, and structurally integral.

## The Human Connection

Transparent architecture isn't just about visibility—it's about connection. The ability to see through walls creates a more open and connected environment, where the boundaries between inside and outside, public and private, begin to dissolve.
