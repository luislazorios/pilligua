Public spaces are the living rooms of our cities. When designed with geometric precision, they create a sense of order and calm that invites people to gather, reflect, and connect.

## The Geometry of Harmony

Urban spaces designed with geometric principles create a sense of order and calm. The repetition of forms, the play of light and shadow, and the careful consideration of scale all contribute to the experience of a space.

### Key Principles

1. **Rhythm** - Repetition creates visual harmony
2. **Proportion** - Scale relative to human movement
3. **Materiality** - Textures that invite touch
